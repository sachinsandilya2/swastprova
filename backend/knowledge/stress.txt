SWASTPROVA KNOWLEDGE: STRESS

Stress is a response that can happen when a person feels pressure,
demands, uncertainty or difficulty coping with a situation.

Common experiences associated with stress may include:
- Feeling overwhelmed
- Irritability
- Difficulty concentrating
- Restlessness
- Changes in sleep
- Feeling mentally tired

General supportive strategies:
- Break large tasks into smaller steps
- Take short breaks
- Maintain a regular sleep routine
- Talk to someone trusted
- Do light physical activity when appropriate
- Try slow breathing or relaxation exercises
- Focus on one manageable task at a time

If stress is persistent, severe, or significantly affects daily life,
encourage the user to speak with a qualified mental-health professional.

Do not diagnose a stress-related disorder.
