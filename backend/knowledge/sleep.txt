SWASTPROVA KNOWLEDGE: SLEEP

Healthy sleep supports physical health, emotional wellbeing,
learning, concentration and daily functioning.

General sleep-support habits:
- Keep a consistent sleep and wake schedule
- Create a calm and comfortable sleep environment
- Reduce stimulating activities close to bedtime
- Avoid excessive caffeine, especially later in the day
- Keep regular daytime activity
- Use a relaxing bedtime routine
- Avoid repeatedly checking the clock if it increases worry

If sleep difficulties continue for a long period or significantly
affect daytime functioning, encourage the user to speak with a
qualified healthcare professional.

Do not diagnose insomnia or another sleep disorder from chat alone.
