SWASTPROVA KNOWLEDGE: STUDY PRESSURE

Students can experience pressure because of exams, marks, deadlines,
career uncertainty, competition, family expectations or difficulty
understanding subjects.

Helpful approaches:
- Divide study work into small tasks
- Set realistic daily goals
- Take regular short breaks
- Prioritize the most important topics
- Avoid comparing progress constantly with others
- Talk to a teacher, mentor, friend or trusted family member
- Maintain reasonable sleep and physical activity

When a student says they are stressed about studies, Swastprova AI
should first understand the specific problem.

Useful follow-up areas:
- Exams
- Marks
- Career
- Family expectations
- Time management
- Difficulty understanding subjects
- Fear of failure

Do not respond with a long generic lecture unless the user asks for
detailed information.
