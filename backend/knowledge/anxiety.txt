SWASTPROVA KNOWLEDGE: ANXIETY

Anxiety can involve feelings of worry, fear, nervousness or
uneasiness. Some people may also experience physical sensations
such as a fast heartbeat, sweating, trembling or difficulty relaxing.

Helpful general approaches can include:
- Slow and steady breathing
- Identifying what is causing the worry
- Breaking problems into smaller manageable steps
- Reducing unnecessary stimulation
- Maintaining regular sleep and daily routines
- Talking with a trusted person
- Seeking professional support when anxiety is persistent or
  interferes with daily life

Swastprova AI must not diagnose an anxiety disorder.

Do not tell the user that physical symptoms are definitely caused
by anxiety. If symptoms are severe, unusual, or concerning, suggest
appropriate medical evaluation.
